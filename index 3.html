Inventory
Tap an item, then tap another slot to move or swap. Select a stack, then spread two fingers apart anywhere on the inventory to split it in half. The other half stays in its slot. Two-finger tap on a selected stack picks up one item. Craft logs into planks, planks into sticks, or four planks into a crafting table. Demo blocks are illustrative icons.
Blocks loaded in preview. Open in a browser to move and craft items.

Original demonstration icons and character illustration. Not connected to Minecraft. Real inventory control requires a PC-side Minecraft Java mod.
